About

DJ Kingkale makes amapiano and hip-hop built on the log drum, soulful chord work, and lyrics that stay close to the ground — hardship, hustle, and the climb out of it. Part of the SoftBoys Association collective.

The catalog so far moves between two poles: GIFTED, a record about finding upliftment even in hard trading conditions, and NJALO, a heartbreak-tinted amapiano cut carried by piano and space.
